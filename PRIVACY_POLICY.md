# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 4, 2026**

## Introduction

Earned ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Email address, name, and profile photo when you create an account
- **Goals and Habits**: Personal goals and habits you enter during onboarding
- **Task Data**: Tasks you create, complete, and track
- **Rewards Data**: Rewards you create and redeem

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the app (task completions, reward redemptions)
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, and app version
- **Analytics**: Anonymous usage statistics to improve the app

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the Earned app service
- Personalize your experience with AI-generated tasks and rewards
- Track your progress and calculate streaks
- Send notifications about your tasks and rewards
- Improve our app and develop new features
- Communicate with you about updates and support

## Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform)
- We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest
- Authentication is handled through secure providers (Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In)
- We never store your passwords - authentication is handled by third-party providers

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Firebase** (Google): Authentication, database, and analytics
- **OpenAI**: AI-powered task and reward generation
- **Apple**: Sign-In with Apple authentication
- **Google**: Google Sign-In authentication

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update or correct your information
- **Deletion**: Delete your account and all associated data
- **Export**: Export your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, use the Settings page in the app or contact us at support@my-bots.uz.

## Data Retention

- Active account data is retained while your account is active
- When you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted within 30 days
- Anonymous analytics data may be retained for up to 2 years

## Children's Privacy

Earned is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. By using Earned, you consent to such transfers.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Sending you a notification for significant changes

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
- **Email**: support@my-bots.uz
